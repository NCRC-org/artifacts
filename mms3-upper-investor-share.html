Upper-Income and Investor Buyers as a Share of Home Purchase Loans, 2020-2025

Upper-income owner-occupants and investors combined, as a share of all home purchase loans.

Upper income means a borrower income of at least 120% of the area median family income. Investor means a mortgage-financed second home or investment property. Shares are of all home purchase loans, including a small number with no reported income. The 2025 figure is not directly comparable with earlier years because HUD changed how it sets area median income that year.
