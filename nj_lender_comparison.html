Red subject % = lender's lending share falls significantly below NJ population share for that group (threshold: 3+ percentage points below).
Red difference = subject underperforms peer on key equity metrics (Hispanic, Black, LMIB, LMICT, MMCT). Green difference = outperforms peer.
NJ Pop. column = population benchmarks from HUD Low-Mod Income Summary Data (ACS 2020) and Census ACS demographics.

Data Source: HMDA (Home Mortgage Disclosure Act) via FFIEC, stored in BigQuery table justdata-ncrc.dataexplorer.de_hmda. Population: HUD ACS 2020 Low-Mod file + Census ACS.

Filters Applied:
· Geography: All counties in New Jersey
· Years: 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024
· Action Taken: Originations only (action_taken = '1') — not applications, denials, or other dispositions
· Loan Purpose: Home purchase only (loan_purpose = '1') — excludes refinance and home improvement
· Occupancy: Owner-occupied / principal residence (occupancy_type = '1')
· Property Type: 1-4 family units — excludes multifamily 5+ units
· Construction: Site-built only — excludes manufactured housing
· Reverse Mortgage: Excluded

Denominators: Race/ethnicity % uses loans with reported demographic data. LMIB/LMICT/MMCT % uses total originations.
Population benchmarks: Race from Census ACS. LMIB from HUD Low-Mod Summary (ACS 2020): 39.0% of NJ pop earns <80% AMFI. LMICT from FFIEC Census (hdma1-242116.geo.census): 29.1% of NJ pop lives in FFIEC-classified Low (income_level=1, MFI <50%) + Moderate (income_level=2, MFI 50-80%) tracts. MMCT from FFIEC Census: 42.1% of NJ pop lives in tracts where 50%+ of residents are non-white.

SELECT h.lei, MAX(h.lender_name) AS lender_name, MAX(h.lender_type) AS lender_type, h.activity_year, COUNT(*) AS total_originations, COUNTIF(h.is_hispanic) AS hispanic, COUNTIF(h.is_black) AS black, COUNTIF(h.is_white) AS white, COUNTIF(h.is_asian) AS asian, COUNTIF(h.is_native_american) AS native_american, COUNTIF(h.is_multi_racial) AS multi_racial, COUNTIF(h.is_lmib) AS lmib, COUNTIF(h.is_lmict) AS lmict, COUNTIF(h.is_mmct) AS mmct, COUNTIF(h.has_demographic_data) AS loans_with_demo FROM `justdata-ncrc.dataexplorer.de_hmda` h WHERE h.county_state LIKE '%, New Jersey' AND h.activity_year IN (2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024) AND h.action_taken = '1' -- Originations only AND h.occupancy_type = '1' -- Owner-occupied AND h.loan_purpose = '1' -- Home purchase AND h.total_units IN ('1','2','3','4') -- 1-4 family AND h.construction_method = '1' -- Site-built AND (h.reverse_mortgage IS NULL OR h.reverse_mortgage != '1') -- Exclude reverse GROUP BY h.lei, h.activity_year ORDER BY h.activity_year, total_originations DESC
