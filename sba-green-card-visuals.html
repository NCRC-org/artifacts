Methodology and sources

What the map shows

For fiscal year 2025 (October 1, 2024 to September 30, 2025), the number of 7(a) and 504 loans, loan dollars and jobs supported that went to businesses with green-card-holder owners in each of SBA's ten regions. If green-card holders are barred from the programs, these are the amounts at risk. The map also shows the share of each region's total SBA 7(a) and 504 lending that those loans represent, which is how exposed each region is relative to its own SBA activity. Every state takes its region's figure. No number on this page is a state estimate.

Reading the figures as expected losses

The figures measure exposure, not a forecast. Treating them as annual losses assumes that:

  • FY25 volume repeats in future years.
  • Every FY25 borrower with a green-card-holder owner would become ineligible, with no ownership restructuring and no change of status.
  • None of that lending is replaced, whether by other SBA borrowers or by credit from outside SBA. Some of these businesses may still find other financing, probably on different terms.
  • Jobs are the figure lenders reported as created or retained by each loan. They are jobs supported by the lending at risk, not a count of jobs that would be lost.

Sources

  • Green-card counts by region. The Senate and House Small Business Committee minority (Ranking Members Markey and Velázquez) fact sheet, "Congressional Review Act (CRA) Resolution on SBA's Draconian Citizenship Restrictions." It reports loans, loan dollars and jobs for each SBA region. The 504 figures come from the Senate committee's analysis. The 7(a) figures are the committee's estimates, scaled up from the 504 analysis.
  • All SBA lending, for the denominators. SBA's 7(a) and 504 FOIA loan data on the SBA open data portal, as of June 30, 2026. Files used: 7(a) FY2020 to present and 504 FY2010 to present. Field definitions are in the FOIA data dictionary.

How the shares are calculated

  1. Keep every loan in the FOIA files with an approval date from October 1, 2024 to September 30, 2025. That is 78,078 7(a) loans and 6,762 504 loans, 84,840 in all.
  2. Assign each loan to an SBA region by its project state, using the region lists in the committee's table. Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands are in Region 2, and Guam and American Samoa are in Region 9.
  3. Sum loans, jobs and loan dollars for each region. For 504 loans, dollars are the SBA and certified development company amount plus the third-party lender amount, because that is the basis that reproduces the committee's 504 percentages.
  4. Divide the committee's green-card figure for each region by that region's total from step 3.

The national shares are 7.3% of loans, 9.9% of dollars and 9.6% of jobs. As a check, the same method gives 456 of 6,762 FY25 504 loans, or 6.7%, and about $2.0 billion of $18.7 billion in 504 dollars, or about 10.7%, which matches the committee's stated 6.7% and 10%.

Limits to keep in mind

  • SBA's public files have no citizenship or immigration-status field, so green-card loans cannot be identified from them. The numerators come only from the committee.
  • The 7(a) numbers are estimates, so the 7(a) and combined figures are only as good as that estimate.
  • The denominators count every FY25 approval in the file, including about 14,000 7(a) approvals later cancelled. This assumes the committee counted approvals the same way.
  • Region assignment by project state is our assumption. The committee does not say how it assigned loans to regions.
  • The committee lists 266 loans for both Region 7 and Region 8, which may be a transcription error. Its region rows also add to 6,166 loans and 80,215 jobs, against printed totals of 6,165 and 80,213. These should be confirmed with the committee.
  • The loan files are as of June 30, 2026, so some FY25 approvals may have changed status since the committee's analysis.
